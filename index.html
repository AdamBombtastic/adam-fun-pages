A NOTE ON OUR NUMBERS

The very unscientific science.

These are informed ballpark estimates, not experimentally measured counts. The page uses a consistent volume model to give the story scale:

Estimated count ≈ (bowl volume × packing fraction) ÷ item volume

Real quantities depend on variety, size, shape, moisture, how contents are arranged, and whether a bowl is heaped. The 3D contents are illustrative samples—not one rendered mesh per grain.

Especially for crickets and apples, take the number as an invitation to wonder, not a scientific claim.
